================================================================================
3. WILD PAY (pago por aparicion en grid)
================================================================================

  Cuando aparecen 3+ Wilds en cualquier posicion del grid, se cobra un
  pago adicional (independiente de las lineas) antes de entrar al bonus.

  Wilds en grid   Wild Pay (x totalBet)
  -------------   ---------------------
  3               2x
  4               5x
  5               20x

  Wild Pay RTP:
    = (78,030 x 2 + 6,885 x 5 + 243 x 20) / 3,200,000
    = (156,060 + 34,425 + 4,860) / 3,200,000
    = 195,345 / 3,200,000
    = 6.1045%

  Desglose:
    3 wilds:  78,030 x 2  / 3,200,000 = 4.8769%
    4 wilds:   6,885 x 5  / 3,200,000 = 1.0758%
    5 wilds:     243 x 20 / 3,200,000 = 0.1519%


================================================================================
4. BONUS: COFRES CON MULTIPLICADORES
================================================================================

  Se activa con 3+ Wilds en el grid. El tier del bonus depende del
  numero de Wilds que activaron el trigger.

  Estructura: siempre 5 cofres. Calavera = fin del bonus.
  5 Wilds = premio garantizado (sin calaveras).

  Tier  Wilds  Cofres premio  Calaveras  Multiplicadores (x totalBet)
  ----  -----  -------------  ---------  ----------------------------
  0     3      3              2          8x, 18x, 28x
  1     4      4              1          8x, 15x, 20x, 27x
  2     5      5              0          8x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 27x

  Mecanica: el jugador elige cofres uno a uno. Si abre una calavera,
  el bonus termina. Si abre todos los premios, el bonus termina.
  Los cofres se barajan aleatoriamente al inicio.

  Calculo de E[picks] y EV por tier:

  TIER 0 (3 premios, 2 calaveras):
    Distribucion de resultados:
      P(0 premios) = 2/5 = 40%
      P(1 premio)  = 3/10 = 30%
      P(2 premios) = 2/10 = 20%
      P(3 premios) = 1/10 = 10%
    E[picks] = 0x0.4 + 1x0.3 + 2x0.2 + 3x0.1 = 1.0
    P(premio_i cobrado) = 1/3
    EV = pool/3 = 54/3 = 18x totalBet

  TIER 1 (4 premios, 1 calavera):
    Distribucion de resultados:
      P(0 premios) = 1/5 = 20%
      P(1 premio)  = 1/5 = 20%
      P(2 premios) = 1/5 = 20%
      P(3 premios) = 1/5 = 20%
      P(4 premios) = 1/5 = 20%
    E[picks] = (0+1+2+3+4)/5 = 2.0
    P(premio_i cobrado) = 1/2
    EV = pool/2 = 70/2 = 35x totalBet

  TIER 2 (5 premios, 0 calaveras):
    100% cobra todos los premios
    E[picks] = 5.0
    EV = pool = 80x totalBet

  Bonus RTP:
    = (78,030 x 18 + 6,885 x 35 + 243 x 80) / 3,200,000
    = (1,404,540 + 240,975 + 19,440) / 3,200,000
    = 1,664,955 / 3,200,000
    = 52.0298%

  Desglose:
    Bonus 3 wilds:  78,030 x 18 / 3,200,000 = 43.8919%
    Bonus 4 wilds:   6,885 x 35 / 3,200,000 =  7.5305%
    Bonus 5 wilds:     243 x 80 / 3,200,000 =  0.6075%


================================================================================
5. BUY BONUS
================================================================================

  El Buy Bonus permite acceder directamente al wild pay + bonus sin
  necesidad de hacer un giro base. El precio incluye una prima sobre
  el EV esperado (RTP del buy = 95.24%).

  Opcion   Simula    EV total (wild pay+bonus)  Precio (x totalBet)  RTP Buy
  ------   ------    -------------------------  -------------------  -------
  Tier 1   3 Wilds   2 + 18 = 20x               21x                  95.24%
  Tier 2   4 Wilds   5 + 35 = 40x               42x                  95.24%
  Tier 3   5 Wilds   20 + 80 = 100x             105x                 95.24%

  Formula precio: EV / 0.9524
  RTP Buy Bonus: 95.24% — ligeramente inferior al RTP base (96.12%)
  como prima por conveniencia de acceso directo al bonus.

================================================================================

  Componente          Calculo                             RTP
  ----------------    ---------------------------------   ---------
  Lineas              (brute-force 3.2M combos)           37.9888%

  Wild Pay 4W          6,885 x 5  / 3,200,000             1.0758%
  Wild Pay 5W            243 x 20 / 3,200,000             0.1519%
  WILD PAY SUBTOTAL                                        6.1045%

  Bonus 5W               243 x 80 / 3,200,000             0.6075%
  BONUS SUBTOTAL                                          52.0298%
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  TOTAL               37.9888 + 6.1045 + 52.0298       = 96.1232%

  Hit Rate:           46.64% (calculado por brute-force exacto)

7. EJEMPLO COMPLETO — totalBet = 1.00 EUR
================================================================================

  Grid resultante del giro:

    Reel     1        2        3        4        5
    Top:     Queen    Wolf     King     Ace      Jack
    Mid:     King     King     WILD     King     Queen
    Bot:     Wolf     WILD     Ace      WILD     King

  3 Wilds en el grid (Reel2-Bot, Reel3-Mid, Reel4-Bot).

  --- Paso 1: Lineas ---

  Payline 1 [1,1,1,1,1] (fila central): King, King, Wild, King, Queen
    Base: King (primer no-wild)
    Consecutivos: King, King, Wild(=King), King = 4 consecutivos
    Queen rompe la cadena
    Pago: King x4 = 1.65 x 1.00 = 1.65 EUR

  (Resto de paylines: sin mas coincidencias de x3+ en este ejemplo)

  Total lineas = 1.65 EUR

  --- Paso 2: Wild Pay ---

  3 Wilds en grid -> wild pay: 2 x 1.00 = 2.00 EUR

  --- Paso 3: Bonus (Tier 0) ---

  Se activa por 3 Wilds.
  Cofres (mezclados al azar): [8x, 18x, 28x, skull, skull]

  Pick   Cofre    Resultado        Acumulado
  ----   -----    ---------        ---------
  1      #3       18x = 18.00 EUR  18.00 EUR
  2      #5       SKULL            GAME OVER

  Total bonus = 18.00 EUR

  --- Resumen ---

  Concepto                 Importe
  --------                 -------
  Lineas                    1.65 EUR
  Wild Pay (3 Wilds)        2.00 EUR
  Bonus (1 cofre abierto)  18.00 EUR
  TOTAL GANADO             21.65 EUR
  Apuesta (totalBet)        1.00 EUR
  MULTIPLICADOR TIRADA     21.65x
