3. WILD PAY (grid appearance payout)
================================================================================

  When 3+ Wilds appear anywhere on the grid, an additional payout is awarded
  (independent of paylines) before entering the bonus.

  Wilds on grid   Wild Pay (x totalBet)
  -------------   ---------------------
  3               8x
  4               21x
  5               83x

  Wild Pay RTP:
    = (227,070 x 8 + 11,745 x 21 + 243 x 83) / 33,554,432
    = (1,816,560 + 246,645 + 20,169) / 33,554,432
    = 2,083,374 / 33,554,432
    = 6.2089%

  Breakdown:
    3 wilds:  227,070 x 8  / 33,554,432 = 5.4138%
    4 wilds:   11,745 x 21 / 33,554,432 = 0.7351%
    5 wilds:      243 x 83 / 33,554,432 = 0.0601%


================================================================================
4. BONUS: CHESTS WITH MULTIPLIERS
================================================================================

  Triggered by 3+ Wilds on the grid. The bonus tier depends on the
  number of Wilds that activated the trigger.

  Structure: always 5 chests. Skull = end of bonus.
  5 Wilds = guaranteed prize (no skulls).

  Tier  Wilds  Prize chests  Skulls  Multipliers (x totalBet)
  ----  -----  ------------  ------  ----------------------------
  0     3      3             2       25x, 75x, 125x
  1     4      4             1       30x, 60x, 90x, 120x
  2     5      5             0       20x, 40x, 60x, 80x, 100x

  Mechanics: the player picks chests one at a time. If a skull is opened,
  the bonus ends. If all prizes are opened, the bonus ends.
  Chests are randomly shuffled at the start.

  E[picks] and EV calculation per tier:

  TIER 0 (3 prizes, 2 skulls):
    Outcome distribution:
      P(0 prizes) = 2/5 = 40%
      P(1 prize)  = 3/10 = 30%
      P(2 prizes) = 2/10 = 20%
      P(3 prizes) = 1/10 = 10%
    E[picks] = 0x0.4 + 1x0.3 + 2x0.2 + 3x0.1 = 1.0
    P(prize_i collected) = 1/3
    EV = pool/3 = 225/3 = 75x totalBet

  TIER 1 (4 prizes, 1 skull):
    Outcome distribution:
      P(0 prizes) = 1/5 = 20%
      P(1 prize)  = 1/5 = 20%
      P(2 prizes) = 1/5 = 20%
      P(3 prizes) = 1/5 = 20%
      P(4 prizes) = 1/5 = 20%
    E[picks] = (0+1+2+3+4)/5 = 2.0
    P(prize_i collected) = 1/2
    EV = pool/2 = 300/2 = 150x totalBet

  TIER 2 (5 prizes, 0 skulls):
    100% collects all prizes
    E[picks] = 5.0
    EV = pool = 300x totalBet

  Bonus RTP:
    = (227,070 x 75 + 11,745 x 150 + 243 x 300) / 33,554,432
    = (17,030,250 + 1,761,750 + 72,900) / 33,554,432
    = 18,864,900 / 33,554,432
    = 56.2218%

  Breakdown:
    Bonus 3 wilds:  227,070 x 75  / 33,554,432 = 50.7541%
    Bonus 4 wilds:   11,745 x 150 / 33,554,432 =  5.2504%
    Bonus 5 wilds:      243 x 300 / 33,554,432 =  0.2173%


================================================================================
5. BUY BONUS
================================================================================

  Buy Bonus grants direct access to wild pay + bonus without needing
  a base spin. The price includes a premium over the expected EV
  (buy RTP ~ 95%).

  Option   Simulates  EV total (wild pay+bonus)  Price (x totalBet)  Buy RTP
  ------   ---------  -------------------------  ------------------  -------
  Tier 1   3 Wilds     8 + 75  =  83x              88x               94.32%
  Tier 2   4 Wilds    21 + 150 = 171x             180x               95.00%

  Tier 3 (5 Wilds) is not offered: with 0 skulls the outcome is deterministic
  (always 383x), so the player would lose 20x on every purchase.

  Price formula: ceil(EV / 0.9524)
  Buy Bonus RTP: ~95% — slightly below base RTP (96.14%)
  as a convenience premium for direct bonus access.

7. WORKED EXAMPLE — totalBet = 1.00 EUR
================================================================================

  Resulting grid:

    Reel     1        2        3        4        5
    Top:     Queen    Wolf     King     Ace      Jack
    Mid:     King     King     WILD     King     Queen
    Bot:     Wolf     WILD     Ace      WILD     King

  3 Wilds on grid (Reel2-Bot, Reel3-Mid, Reel4-Bot).

  --- Step 1: Lines ---

  Payline 1 [1,1,1,1,1] (middle row): King, King, Wild, King, Queen
    Base: King (first non-wild)
    Consecutive: King, King, Wild(=King), King = 4 consecutive
    Queen breaks the chain
    Payout: King x4 = 6.41 x 1.00 = 6.41 EUR

  (Remaining paylines: no additional x3+ matches in this example)

  Total lines = 6.41 EUR

  --- Step 2: Wild Pay ---

  3 Wilds on grid -> wild pay: 8 x 1.00 = 8.00 EUR

  --- Step 3: Bonus (Tier 0) ---

  Triggered by 3 Wilds.
  Chests (shuffled randomly): [25x, 75x, 125x, skull, skull]

  Pick   Chest    Result           Accumulated
  ----   -----    ---------        -----------
  1      #3       75x = 75.00 EUR  75.00 EUR
  2      #5       SKULL            GAME OVER

  Total bonus = 75.00 EUR

  --- Summary ---

  Item                     Amount
  --------                 -------
  Lines                     6.41 EUR
  Wild Pay (3 Wilds)        8.00 EUR
  Bonus (1 chest opened)   75.00 EUR
  TOTAL WON                89.41 EUR
  Bet (totalBet)            1.00 EUR
  SPIN MULTIPLIER          89.41x
