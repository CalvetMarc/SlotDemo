# Debug Shortcuts

These keyboard shortcuts are handled in `src/Core/Game/Screens/BaseScreen/views/slot-machine-view.ts`.

- `1`: Force line 1 win with symbol `1.png`
- `2`: Force line 2 win with symbol `2.png`
- `3`: Force line 3 win with symbol `3.png`
- `4`: Force line 4 win with symbol `J.png`
- `5`: Force line 5 win with symbol `Q.png`
- `6`: Force line 6 win with symbol `K.png`
- `7`: Force line 7 win with symbol `A.png`
- `8`: Force line 1 win with symbol `Wild_01.png`
- `9`: Force bonus trigger (3 scatters, no line win)

Notes:
- Shortcuts are ignored while reels are spinning.
- They run through the normal spin stop/win presentation flow.
